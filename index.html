About Bellevue

A club with character,
built around the game.

Bellevue is a welcoming home for social, competitive and coaching tennis. Our courts bring together long-standing members, new players, families and league teams in a friendly club environment.

Whether you are returning to tennis, looking for coaching, regular competition or simply want a place to play, there is a court and a community waiting for you.

Tennis coaching

Professional coaching at Bellevue.

Robyn Jones (née Robyn Field) offers private and group tennis coaching at Bellevue Lawn Tennis Club for players aged five and older, from beginner to advanced level.

A former South African professional player, Robyn played American collegiate tennis for the University of Texas Longhorns and competed professionally from 1988 to 1991. She qualified for the main draw at Wimbledon in 1990 and also appeared in the French Open and US Open doubles main draws.
